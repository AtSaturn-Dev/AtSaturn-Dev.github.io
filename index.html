A bit about me

Who I am

I’m Eduardo, a developer and pixel artist from Brazil. Online, I go by Saturn, OrdinaryCicada, or oddpotatodev.

I make things because I like seeing an idea turn into something I can play with. A lot of that means trial and error in Godot: tinkering with shaders, rendering, and little pixel art experiments until something clicks.

Away from the screen, I’m usually spending time with my girlfriend or looking after our little zoo: Batata, our dog, and our cats, Mya and Salem.
